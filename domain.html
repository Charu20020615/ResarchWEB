Research problem and impact

Irregular municipal waste collection creates significant public inconvenience.

It affects pedestrians, schoolchildren, healthcare workers, and local businesses, and it erodes civic confidence.

Impact of irregular waste collection by area
AreaImpact
SocialErodes civic confidence and reduces quality of life.
EnvironmentalSpillage contaminates waterways and violates the National Environmental Act.
EconomicMunicipalities waste fuel on inefficient routes, and nearby businesses lose 15–30% of foot traffic.
TechnicalExisting smart bins only measure fill level. They miss scattered waste, odours, and pedestrian avoidance behaviour.
Public healthAccumulated organic waste breeds dengue-carrying mosquitoes, a correlation confirmed by the Dengue Control Division.

The project aligns with SDG 11 · Sustainable Cities SDG 3 · Good Health SDG 13 · Climate Action

Key references: Subasinghe 2024, Knutsson 2021, IGES 2023, Chathurangika 2024, Abdullah 2024, Kumar 2024, Chen 2024, UN-Habitat 2023.

Existing research and limitations

Current waste technologies mostly follow fixed schedules or use expensive ultrasonic sensors that only monitor how full a bin is. They ignore waste type, spillage outside the bin, and ambient conditions.

IoT research uses gas and humidity sensors, and some studies use computer vision to categorise waste, but current systems do not bring these elements together in a single setup.

Proposed solution architecture

Vision-calibrated fill estimation

DeepLab-lite and MobileNet-UNet models run on edge devices (Raspberry Pi or Jetson Nano) and are benchmarked against ultrasonic sensors.

Improper Disposal Severity Index

YOLO or SSD frameworks with polygon masking classify spatial zones (bin, 0–1 m, 1–3 m) and track persistent littering of local waste items such as lunch sheets and coconut shells, while preserving privacy.

Edge waste typing and spoilage prediction

Transfer learning (MobileNet) combines with sensor fusion (DHT22 temperature and humidity, MQ-135 gas) to classify organic waste and model odour severity.

Event-aware waste demand forecasting

Time-series models (ARIMA, LSTM, XGBoost) use local calendar features such as Poya days and festivals, plus location metadata, to optimise collection schedules.

Domain expertise, knowledge, and data

The team builds expertise in computer vision, IoT sensor fusion, and predictive analytics through academic study and stakeholder consultations with municipal officers and health inspectors, to understand operational workflows and dengue hotspots.

Data collection

Anonymised, motion-triggered images of bin interiors and surroundings are captured and synchronised with environmental sensor readings. Public datasets (TrashNet, TACO) and historical municipal collection logs augment them.

Privacy and ethics

Cameras focus strictly on waste zones to minimise facial capture. Any incidental human faces are blurred during preprocessing.

Objectives and novelties by team member

Member 1 · Ranaweera K U I · IT22194312

Vision-based hygienic risk assessment and species-aware animal deterrent

A Teachable Machine binary classifier separates organic from non-organic waste. Organic content, temperature, and time combine into a hygienic risk score. A second classifier recognises stray dogs, cats, and cattle, and a graduated, species-specific ultrasonic deterrent (18–25 kHz) runs only when risk is high, so animals do not habituate and scatter the waste.

Member 2 · Edirisinghe E A · IT22200006

Motion-triggered camera-based fill level detection

A PIR motion sensor triggers image capture, and a TFLite edge model trained on 500+ augmented images across several fill levels classifies the result. This removes the need for continuous ultrasonic sensing, cutting power use and data volume while giving finer-grained fill insight.

Member 3 · Herath H M A M · IT22296214

Event-aware waste demand forecasting

A spatio-temporal model combines calendar events (Poya days, school terms, Fridays) with location profiles (temples, schools) to predict waste spikes. A decision-support interface turns the forecast into advance alerts, so municipalities can adjust schedules or place temporary bins instead of following static routes.

Member 4 · Ranasinghe M J N · IT22262318

Motion-triggered improper disposal and litter severity detection

Existing systems ignore the area outside the bin. A Teachable Machine image classifier, trained on clean, moderate, and severe litter, identifies and quantifies improper disposal in the zones around the bin.
