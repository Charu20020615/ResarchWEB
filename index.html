R26-IT-084 · Final year research 2026 · SLIIT

Public bins that see more than how full they are.

Irregular waste collection inconveniences pedestrians, schoolchildren, healthcare workers, and local businesses. Existing smart bins only measure fill level. VisionWaste adds edge cameras, environmental sensing, and event-aware forecasting to assess hygiene risk, detect scattered litter, deter stray animals, and plan collections.

Overview

What the system is for

Research problem

Existing smart bins only measure how full they are.

They cannot detect scattered waste, odours, or pedestrians avoiding a street. Irregular collection erodes civic confidence, pollutes waterways, wastes fuel on inefficient routes, and lets organic waste breed dengue-carrying mosquitoes.

Impact areas

  • Social Lower civic confidence and quality of life.
  • Environmental Spillage contaminates waterways and breaches the National Environmental Act.
  • Economic Wasted fuel and lost foot traffic for local businesses.
  • Public health Organic waste breeds dengue-carrying mosquitoes.

Method

From sensing to action

Read the proposed solution

  1. 01SenseA PIR sensor triggers image capture. DHT22 and MQ-135 sensors read temperature, humidity, and gas.
  2. 02Classify at the edgeLightweight TFLite and MobileNet models run on a Raspberry Pi or Jetson Nano.
  3. 03Fuse and scoreWaste type, weather, and time give a hygienic risk score, alongside fill level and litter severity.
  4. 04Forecast and actEvent-aware forecasts raise advance alerts, and a graduated deterrent runs only at high risk.
