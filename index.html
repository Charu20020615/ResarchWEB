Research project · R26-IT-084

VisionWaste

Vision-based hygienic risk prediction and animal deterrence for public waste bins.

VisionWaste watches public bins with cameras and phones. From one image it classifies waste, detects animals, estimates fill level, and can score litter outside the bin. A rule-based engine combines those signals with weather, then supports staff alerts, audio cues, forecasts, and collection planning. Citizens can look up a nearby bin.

Overview

What the system is for

Research problem

Avoiding inconvenience to people due to irregular waste.

The project treats irregular public-bin waste as a combined sensing problem: organic waste, animals at the bin, fill level, litter outside the bin, and weather. The risk level is produced by explicit rules so a dashboard can show why an alert was raised.

Scope on record

  • Waste Organic or non-organic, MobileNetV2.
  • Animals Cat, crow, dog, and monkey, YOLOv8.
  • Fill Empty, half, or overflow, YOLOv8n.
  • Litter Outside-bin litter severity index, YOLO11.
  • Action Frame-level littering-event detector, YOLO11.

Method

From image to action

Read the full methodology

  1. 01CaptureA JPEG arrives from the ESP32-CAM, the phone app, or an admin upload.
  2. 02InferThe Express gateway calls separate model services. It does not run the models itself.
  3. 03DecideWaste type, animals, and weather feed a documented rule table.
  4. 04RespondCaptures, alerts, optional audio, maps, and collection stops.
