Team

The research group

VisionWaste is built by four Faculty of Computing students at SLIIT (group R26-IT-084). Each member owns one novelty, and together they make irregular public-bin waste easier to see, explain, and act on.

Research team

Built by SLIIT final year researchers

Four components, one system: the bin interior, its surroundings, and the collection schedule.

RK

Member 1 · IT22194312

Ranaweera K U I

Vision-based hygienic risk assessment and species-aware animal deterrent. A Teachable Machine binary classifier separates organic from non-organic waste. Organic content, temperature, and time combine into a hygienic risk score. A second classifier recognises stray dogs, cats, and cattle, and a graduated, species-specific ultrasonic deterrent (18–25 kHz) runs only when risk is high, so animals do not habituate and scatter the waste.

ranaweeraimashi880@gmail.com

EE

Member 2 · IT22200006

Edirisinghe E A

Motion-triggered camera-based fill level detection. A PIR motion sensor triggers image capture, and a TFLite edge model trained on 500+ augmented images across several fill levels classifies the result. This removes the need for continuous ultrasonic sensing, cutting power use and data volume while giving finer-grained fill insight.

charukasedirisinghe@gmail.com

HH

Member 3 · IT22296214

Herath H M A M

Event-aware waste demand forecasting. A spatio-temporal model combines calendar events (Poya days, school terms, Fridays) with location profiles (temples, schools) to predict waste spikes. A decision-support interface turns the forecast into advance alerts, so municipalities can adjust schedules or place temporary bins instead of following static routes.

it22296214@my.sliit.lk

RM

Member 4 · IT22262318

Ranasinghe M J N

Motion-triggered improper disposal and litter severity detection. Existing systems ignore the area outside the bin. A Teachable Machine image classifier, trained on clean, moderate, and severe litter, identifies and quantifies improper disposal in the zones around the bin.

janinethminaa2002@gmail.com
